# Delivery and Fulfillment

**Status:** Public documentation draft

Move+ Marketplace uses a fulfillment process for supported physical Real Items purchased or redeemed through the Marketplace.

The current planned model is for Move+ to prepare, pack, and hand eligible physical orders to suitable third-party courier services operating within the Philippines.

Courier selection, delivery fees, service availability, and fulfillment procedures may depend on the product, delivery address, and current operating conditions.

Move+ does not currently provide an automated courier integration or guaranteed delivery schedule.

## Fulfillment model

Move+ is responsible for coordinating the fulfillment of supported physical Marketplace orders.

The planned process may include:

1. Receiving and reviewing the confirmed order.
2. Confirming that the product remains available.
3. Reviewing the submitted recipient and delivery information.
4. Preparing and packing the product.
5. Selecting a suitable local courier.
6. Handing the parcel to the courier.
7. Recording the courier and tracking information.
8. Monitoring the delivery status where information is available.
9. Coordinating delivery issues when necessary.
10. Marking the order as delivered, returned, failed, or cancelled as appropriate.

Move+ may contact the customer when clarification or additional delivery coordination is required.

## Delivery coverage

Physical Marketplace delivery is intended to be available nationwide within the Philippines.

Actual delivery availability remains subject to:

- Courier serviceability.
- Product restrictions.
- Remote-area limitations.
- Address accessibility.
- Weather or emergency conditions.
- Courier operating schedules.
- Marketplace fulfillment capacity.
- Other operational restrictions.

A Philippine address does not guarantee that every courier or product can be delivered to that location.

Move+ may contact the customer when the submitted location cannot be served through the initially selected courier.

International delivery is not currently available.

## Delivery addresses

Customers must provide accurate and complete delivery information for physical orders.

Required information may include:

- Recipient name.
- Contact number.
- Email address.
- House, unit, building, or lot information.
- Street.
- Barangay.
- City or municipality.
- Province.
- Postal code where applicable.
- Nearby landmark or delivery instructions where needed.

The customer is responsible for reviewing the submitted information before completing checkout.

Incorrect, incomplete, inaccessible, or outdated delivery information may cause:

- Fulfillment delays.
- Failed delivery attempts.
- Return of the parcel.
- Additional coordination.
- Additional redelivery charges.
- Cancellation when delivery cannot reasonably be completed.

Move+ may contact the recipient using the information provided during checkout.

## Delivery fees

Delivery fees may depend on the applicable product and fulfillment arrangement.

Factors may include:

- Product size.
- Product weight.
- Delivery destination.
- Courier pricing.
- Remote-area charges.
- Packaging requirements.
- Redelivery requirements.
- Special handling needs.

The method used to collect delivery fees has not yet been finalized as a universal Marketplace rule.

A product listing or checkout should clearly state the applicable delivery arrangement when that information is available.

Customers should not assume that delivery is free, included in the listed Energy requirement, or included in the stablecoin amount unless the specific checkout clearly states that it is included.

## Order confirmation

An order confirmation means that Move+ Marketplace has recorded the submitted order.

It does not necessarily mean that the product has already been:

- Reviewed for fulfillment.
- Packed.
- Assigned to a courier.
- Shipped.
- Delivered.

An order may remain pending while Move+ confirms product availability, recipient information, payment or Energy eligibility, and fulfillment requirements.

Move+ may contact the customer before shipping when additional information is required.

## Order stages

A physical order may progress through the following stages.

### Order confirmed

The order has been recorded by Move+ Marketplace.

Payment, Energy redemption, product eligibility, and delivery information may still be reviewed before fulfillment continues.

### Preparing

Move+ is reviewing, preparing, or packing the product.

The order has not yet been handed to a courier.

### Ready for courier

The parcel has been prepared and is waiting for courier pickup or handoff.

A tracking number may not yet be available.

### Shipped

The parcel has been handed to the selected courier.

Courier tracking information may become available after the courier registers the shipment.

### Out for delivery

The courier has marked the parcel for delivery to the recipient.

The recipient should remain reachable through the provided contact number.

### Delivered

The courier or Move+ has recorded the parcel as successfully delivered.

### Delivery failed

A delivery attempt was not completed.

Possible reasons may include:

- The recipient was unavailable.
- The contact number could not be reached.
- The address was incomplete or incorrect.
- The location was inaccessible.
- The recipient declined the parcel.
- The courier could not complete the route.
- Other courier or operational issues.

A failed delivery status does not guarantee that the courier will automatically attempt delivery again.

### Returned

The parcel has been or is being returned after an unsuccessful delivery or other fulfillment issue.

The order may require review before any redelivery, replacement, adjustment, or cancellation decision is made.

### Cancelled

The order will no longer continue through the normal fulfillment process.

Cancellation does not automatically guarantee an Energy restoration, stablecoin refund, replacement, or reimbursement.

Any applicable adjustment depends on review of the specific order and payment status.

## Courier selection

Move+ may choose a suitable local third-party courier based on:

- Delivery location.
- Product size and weight.
- Courier serviceability.
- Courier availability.
- Delivery cost.
- Current operating conditions.
- Special product requirements.

The courier used for one order may differ from the courier used for another order.

Marketplace documentation does not guarantee the use of a specific courier company.

Move+ does not currently claim an official automated integration with any named courier service.

## Tracking numbers

Tracking numbers will initially be recorded manually by Move+ after the parcel is registered with the courier.

Where available, the tracking information may be:

- Displayed in an order-related Marketplace interface.
- Sent through an official Move+ communication channel.
- Provided directly to the customer by Move+ support.
- Used on the selected courier’s official tracking service.

A tracking number may not be available immediately after the order enters the Ready for courier or Shipped stage.

Courier tracking updates may also be delayed or incomplete.

The courier’s tracking record should not be treated as a guarantee of a specific delivery date.

## Delivery timing

Move+ does not currently publish fixed delivery-time estimates.

Delivery timing may vary according to:

- Product preparation.
- Order volume.
- Courier pickup schedules.
- Destination.
- Local serviceability.
- Weekends and holidays.
- Weather.
- Transportation disruptions.
- Failed delivery attempts.
- Other operational conditions.

An order confirmation, tracking number, or Shipped status does not guarantee delivery by a specific date.

Any delivery estimate shown for a particular order should be treated as an estimate rather than a guarantee.

## Failed delivery attempts

When a delivery attempt fails, the courier may attempt delivery again according to its own operating procedures.

A retry may depend on:

- Courier policy.
- Remaining delivery attempts.
- Recipient availability.
- Corrected address or contact information.
- Serviceability.
- Additional delivery charges.
- Return status of the parcel.

Customers should respond promptly when Move+ or the courier requests updated delivery information.

Move+ does not guarantee an unlimited number of delivery attempts.

## Redelivery charges

When another delivery attempt creates an additional courier or handling charge, the customer is responsible for the applicable redelivery amount.

A redelivery charge may apply when:

- The recipient was unavailable.
- The customer submitted incorrect or incomplete information.
- The recipient could not be contacted.
- The recipient requested a change after shipment.
- The parcel was returned and must be shipped again.
- The courier applies an additional attempt or remote-area charge.

The available method for collecting redelivery charges has not yet been finalized as a universal Marketplace process.

Move+ should communicate the applicable amount and arrangement before initiating a chargeable redelivery.

Redelivery is not guaranteed when the product, courier service, or delivery location is no longer available.

## Returned parcels

A parcel may be returned to Move+ when delivery cannot be completed.

Returned orders may require review of:

- Parcel condition.
- Product condition.
- Delivery history.
- Reason for return.
- Customer-provided information.
- Courier charges.
- Payment status.
- Energy redemption status.
- Product availability.
- Redelivery feasibility.

A returned parcel does not automatically create a refund, replacement, or free redelivery entitlement.

Move+ may contact the customer to determine the next available action.

## Vouchers

Supported vouchers are intended to be delivered digitally.

Digital voucher delivery may use an official Marketplace or Move+ communication method.

A voucher listing should state the applicable:

- Redemption instructions.
- Validity period.
- Supported location or merchant.
- Usage limitations.
- Recipient requirements.
- Other product-specific terms.

Digital vouchers normally do not require physical courier delivery.

A voucher should not be treated as successfully delivered until the required voucher information or redemption access has been provided through the applicable channel.

## Returns and replacements

The general Marketplace return and replacement process has not yet been finalized.

Customers should not assume that every physical product supports:

- Return for change of mind.
- Automatic replacement.
- Automatic refund.
- Free return shipping.
- Free redelivery.
- Energy restoration.
- Stablecoin reimbursement.

Possible return or replacement requests may require individual review based on:

- Product condition.
- Incorrect product received.
- Damage reported after delivery.
- Packaging condition.
- Courier records.
- Available evidence.
- Product availability.
- Product-specific terms.
- Time elapsed after delivery.

Move+ may publish a separate return and replacement policy before broader Marketplace fulfillment becomes available.

Until such a policy is finalized, customers should contact official Move+ support regarding a specific order issue.

## Damaged or incorrect items

A customer receiving a visibly damaged or incorrect item should contact official Move+ support as soon as reasonably possible.

Move+ may request information such as:

- Order reference.
- Product received.
- Description of the issue.
- Photos or video of the parcel and product.
- Packaging condition.
- Courier information.
- Delivery date.

Providing information does not automatically guarantee a refund or replacement.

The available resolution depends on review of the order, product, courier records, and current Marketplace policy.

## Cancellations

Order cancellation availability depends on the order stage.

Cancellation may no longer be practical after:

- Product preparation.
- Packing.
- Courier assignment.
- Courier handoff.
- Shipment.
- Delivery.
- Use or redemption of a digital voucher.

Move+ does not currently provide a guaranteed self-service cancellation function for every Marketplace order.

Cancellation requests should be raised through an official Move+ support channel and remain subject to review.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Providing accurate recipient information.
- Providing a reachable contact number.
- Providing a complete delivery address.
- Reviewing product and order information.
- Monitoring official order or courier updates.
- Being available during delivery attempts.
- Responding to courier or Move+ communications.
- Paying an applicable redelivery charge when required.
- Reviewing voucher instructions.
- Reporting order issues through official support channels.

Failure to meet these responsibilities may delay or prevent fulfillment.

## Move+ responsibilities

Under the planned fulfillment model, Move+ is responsible for:

- Reviewing supported orders.
- Preparing and packing applicable physical products.
- Selecting a suitable courier.
- Providing tracking information where available.
- Coordinating reasonable fulfillment communication.
- Reviewing reported fulfillment issues.
- Protecting submitted customer information according to the applicable Privacy Policy.

Move+ does not guarantee uninterrupted courier service or delivery under circumstances outside its reasonable control.

## Fulfillment safety

Customers should:

- Use only official Move+ Marketplace surfaces.
- Review recipient details before submitting an order.
- Confirm any tracking number through the selected courier’s official channel.
- Be cautious of fake delivery messages.
- Avoid sending payments to unofficial accounts.
- Verify any requested redelivery charge through official Move+ support.
- Never share wallet seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or one-time security codes with a courier or support account.

Move+ will not request a wallet seed phrase or private key to process delivery.

- The fulfillment model is still being finalized.
- Move+ plans to prepare, pack, and ship supported physical products.
- Delivery will use suitable third-party couriers.
- No named courier partnership or automated courier integration is currently confirmed.
- Delivery fees depend on the product.
- A universal delivery-fee collection method has not been finalized.
- Delivery is intended for the Philippines, subject to courier serviceability.
- International delivery is not currently available.
- Tracking numbers will initially be entered manually.
- Courier tracking updates may be delayed.
- Fixed delivery-time estimates are not currently published.
- Failed delivery retries depend on courier policy and availability.
- Customers may be responsible for additional redelivery charges.
- Returns and replacements are not yet governed by a finalized universal policy.
- Refunds, replacements, Energy restoration, and stablecoin reversals are not automatically guaranteed.
- Vouchers are intended to be delivered digitally.
- Marketplace fulfillment does not currently provide complete automated shipment tracking.

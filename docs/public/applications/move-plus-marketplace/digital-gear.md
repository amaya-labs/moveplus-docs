# Marketplace Digital Gear

**Status:** Public documentation draft

The Digital Gear section of the hosted Move+ Marketplace is currently a preview and discovery experience.

Users may browse selected Move+ Digital Gear collections, review basic collection information, filter previews by supported network or category, and open compatible external pages where available.

The Marketplace Digital Gear section does not currently provide direct NFT purchasing, minting, wallet signing, or asset transfer.

Digital Gear ownership verification, equipping, repair, and progression take place through the supported Move+ Digital Gear experience rather than through the marketplace preview.

## Current marketplace status

Digital Gear may appear as a separate tab in the hosted Move+ Marketplace.

The current preview experience may allow users to:

- Browse numbered Digital Gear previews.
- Filter gear by network or collection.
- Search preview titles and descriptions.
- Open a Digital Gear detail view.
- Review the displayed network and collection.
- Open the Move+ application.
- Follow a compatible external network or marketplace link where available.

The Digital Gear tab may be feature-controlled and may not appear in every marketplace build or environment.

The native Move+ Marketplace tab is currently focused on real-item products and Energy-based redemption. It should not be treated as a live native NFT marketplace.

## Supported networks and collections

The hosted Digital Gear preview currently focuses on selected Move+ collections associated with Ronin and Base.

### Ronin Genesis

The hosted marketplace may display numbered previews from the Move+ Genesis collection on Ronin.

Preview information may include:

- Gear name or number.
- Collection.
- Network.
- Artwork.
- Displayed rarity or design information.
- An external Ronin-related link where available.
- An option to open Move+.

These previews do not represent live marketplace inventory or guarantee that a specific item is available for purchase.

Ronin Season gear, Shoebox products, starter gear, and older collections are not currently presented as fully available hosted Digital Gear listings.

### Base Founder Gear

The hosted marketplace may display numbered previews from the Base Founder Gear collection.

Preview information may include:

- Gear name or number.
- Base network label.
- Founder collection label.
- Artwork or design.
- Displayed utility information.
- An external OpenSea link where available.
- An option to open Move+.

Base Founder Gear contains 100 numbered items.

The presence of a preview card does not guarantee that the item is currently listed, available, or owned by a seller on an external marketplace.

Exact earning values and progression rules are documented separately and should not be inferred only from marketplace display labels.

### Unsupported networks

Celo stablecoin checkout for real items does not mean that Celo Digital Gear is supported.

The hosted Digital Gear preview does not currently present collections from:

- Celo.
- Amaya L1.
- Solana.
- Fuse.
- Other unsupported networks.

Amaya L1 Digital Gear remains a planned direction and is not currently live.

## Browsing and filters

When the Digital Gear tab is available, users may be able to browse using filters such as:

- All.
- Ronin.
- Base.
- Genesis.
- Founder.
- Cycling.

Search may also be available for preview titles, descriptions, networks, collections, designs, or other displayed information.

The Cycling filter currently represents a future category. Cycling Digital Gear is not yet available through the marketplace.

An empty filter result does not necessarily indicate an application error. It may indicate that no supported gear is available under that category.

## Preview information

Digital Gear preview cards may display information such as:

- Gear name.
- Gear number.
- Collection.
- Network.
- Artwork.
- Rarity or collection badge.
- Design information.
- Preview status.
- External-link actions.

Some displayed utility labels may be informational or subject to future configuration changes.

Marketplace preview cards do not currently provide authoritative information about:

- Active ownership.
- Current seller inventory.
- Final sale price.
- Current durability.
- Current gear level.
- Distance progression.
- Repair status.
- Exact earning eligibility.
- Guaranteed multipliers.
- Guaranteed daily earning limits.

Users should confirm current utility inside Move+ after linking and verifying the corresponding wallet.

## Direct purchase and minting

The hosted Marketplace Digital Gear section does not currently support:

- Direct NFT purchasing.
- Direct NFT minting.
- MiniPay NFT checkout.
- Stablecoin payment for Digital Gear.
- Energy payment for Digital Gear.
- ENR payment for Digital Gear.
- RON payment inside the hosted marketplace.
- Wallet signing for NFT transactions.
- Asset transfers.
- Direct smart-contract interaction.

MiniPay and Celo stablecoin checkout currently relate to supported real-item purchases, not Digital Gear transactions.

Any Digital Gear transaction completed through an external marketplace takes place outside the Move+ marketplace preview.

## External marketplace transactions

Some Digital Gear previews may provide an external link.

Depending on the collection, the link may open:

- OpenSea.
- A Ronin network page.
- A compatible external marketplace.
- A blockchain explorer.
- Another approved external collection page.

An external link does not guarantee that purchasing is available.

Users are responsible for reviewing the external page and determining whether it is:

- A marketplace listing.
- A collection page.
- A blockchain explorer page.
- An informational page.
- An unavailable or expired listing.

Move+ does not control external marketplace availability, seller behavior, transaction fees, collection liquidity, or resale value.

## From external acquisition to Move+ utility

When compatible Digital Gear is acquired outside Move+, the general application flow is:

1. Complete the external transaction using the appropriate network wallet.
2. Open Move+.
3. Sign in to the intended Move+ account.
4. Open the Digital Gear or Web3 section.
5. Choose the matching network.
6. Link the wallet that owns the gear.
7. Refresh or sync supported ownership.
8. Verify that the gear appears inside Move+.
9. Select or equip the gear where supported.

Acquiring an asset externally does not guarantee Move+ utility.

The asset must belong to a supported collection and meet the current ownership, network, application, condition, and eligibility requirements.

Detailed ownership verification, equipping, durability, repair, and progression behavior is documented under Move+ Digital Gear.

## Ronin Digital Gear

Compatible Ronin Digital Gear may be used inside Move+ after the Ronin wallet is linked and ownership is verified.

The marketplace preview itself does not perform:

- Ronin wallet ownership verification.
- Gear synchronization.
- Equipping.
- Durability management.
- Repair.
- Upgrade.
- Awakening.
- Energy eligibility checks.

These actions belong to the Move+ Digital Gear and Web3 dashboard experience.

Some Ronin marketplace or network links may lead to external pages. Users should confirm whether the destination supports purchasing or only displays blockchain information.

## Base Founder Gear

Compatible Base Founder Gear may become usable inside Move+ after the Base wallet is linked and ownership is verified.

The hosted marketplace preview may provide an OpenSea link for supported Base Founder Gear pages.

The marketplace preview does not verify:

- Whether the item is currently for sale.
- Whether the displayed external listing remains active.
- Whether the seller controls the item.
- Whether the final transaction price is appropriate.
- Whether the item currently qualifies for all Move+ utility.

Users should confirm current gear utility inside Move+ after ownership verification.

## MiniPay limitations

The MiniPay-compatible marketplace may display the same Digital Gear previews as the hosted web marketplace.

Digital Gear remains preview-only inside the MiniPay environment.

MiniPay does not currently provide:

- Direct Digital Gear checkout.
- NFT minting.
- NFT wallet signing.
- NFT transfer.
- Stablecoin payment for Digital Gear.
- Ownership verification.
- Equipping or progression.

MiniPay stablecoin checkout is currently intended for supported real-item marketplace transactions.

## Cycling Digital Gear

Cycling-related categories may eventually include:

## Accounts and wallets

A Move+ account or blockchain wallet is not generally required to browse hosted Digital Gear previews.

A compatible blockchain wallet is required when the user chooses to complete a transaction through an external blockchain marketplace.

A Move+ account and the matching linked wallet are required to verify and use compatible Digital Gear inside Move+.

The appropriate network must match the gear:

- Ronin wallet for supported Ronin gear.
- Base wallet for supported Base gear.

MiniPay access is not required for Digital Gear preview browsing or Move+ gear management.

## Wallet and transaction safety

Before interacting with an external Digital Gear page, users should verify:

- The selected blockchain network.
- The collection name.
- The item or token number.
- The external website address.
- The connected wallet.
- The transaction details.
- The amount being paid.
- Applicable blockchain network fees.
- The recipient or smart-contract address shown by the wallet.

Users should be cautious of fake collections, impersonation accounts, copied artwork, fraudulent marketplace links, and unsolicited private messages.

Move+ will never request:

- A wallet recovery phrase.
- A seed phrase.
- A private key.
- Remote access to the user’s device.
- A transfer to “verify” wallet ownership.

Blockchain transactions are often irreversible after confirmation.

## Marketplace and utility distinction

The Marketplace Digital Gear section is intended for discovery and preview.

The main Move+ Digital Gear system is responsible for:

- Wallet linking.
- Ownership verification.
- Gear synchronization.
- Equipping.
- Reward eligibility.
- Durability.
- Repair.
- Levels.
- Upgrades.
- Awakening-related progression.

Marketplace visibility should not be interpreted as confirmation that every displayed item currently supports every Move+ feature.

## Current limitations

- Hosted Digital Gear is currently preview-only.
- The Digital Gear tab may be feature-controlled.
- The native Move+ Marketplace tab does not currently provide a live NFT marketplace.
- Direct NFT purchasing is not available through the hosted Digital Gear section.
- MiniPay cannot currently be used to buy Digital Gear.
- Energy, ENR, Celo stablecoins, and RON are not supported as direct Digital Gear marketplace payments.
- NFT minting, signing, and transfer are not available through the preview.
- Ronin Genesis previews do not guarantee purchase availability.
- Ronin Season and Shoebox products are not currently part of the hosted Digital Gear preview.
- Base Founder Gear previews may link to external OpenSea pages.
- External listings and prices are controlled outside Move+.
- Cycling Digital Gear is not currently available.
- Celo and Amaya L1 are not current Digital Gear marketplace networks.
- Marketplace previews do not guarantee earning utility, liquidity, resale value, or financial return.

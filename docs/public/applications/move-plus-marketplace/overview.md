# Move+ Marketplace

**Status:** Public documentation draft

Move+ Marketplace is the fitness-linked commerce section of the Move+ ecosystem.

It connects Move+ activity and Energy utility with selected physical fitness products, Move+ merchandise, partner products, and Digital Gear discovery.

Move+ Marketplace is not currently an open public marketplace where any merchant can create listings. Products are selected and managed through the Move+ marketplace system.

## Relationship with Move+

Move+ users can access marketplace features from the main Move+ application.

The marketplace creates a practical utility path connecting:

1. Supported fitness activity.
2. Eligible Energy rewards.
3. Marketplace access.
4. Selected physical products or Digital Gear discovery.
5. Delivery or external ownership flows where applicable.

A blockchain wallet is not required to browse Real Items.

Eligible users with an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription may use Energy to pay the full cost of supported Real Items without connecting a blockchain wallet.

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may use Energy only as a discount of up to 20% toward an eligible stablecoin checkout. This discount requires verified gear ownership and the matching linked wallet.

Wallet access may also be required for Digital Gear ownership, external blockchain marketplaces, or supported stablecoin checkout experiences.

## Marketplace surfaces

Move+ Marketplace currently includes more than one user-facing surface.

### Native Move+ marketplace

The native marketplace inside the Move+ application is the primary marketplace experience for eligible full-Energy Real Item redemption.

Full Energy payment is limited to users with an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription.

Users may be able to:

- Browse available physical products.
- Filter products by supported category.
- Open product details.
- Review the listed Energy requirement.
- Review the applicable payment eligibility.
- Submit delivery information.
- Redeem eligible products using full Energy when the user has an active qualifying iOS subscription.
- Receive confirmation that the order was recorded for fulfillment.

Product availability, stock, pricing, and eligibility may change.

### Hosted marketplace

Move+ also maintains a hosted web marketplace.

The hosted marketplace may provide:

- Real-item catalog browsing.
- Product details and cart functionality.
- Digital Gear previews.
- MiniPay-compatible stablecoin checkout where enabled.
- Energy-linked discounts where supported.
- Links to compatible external Digital Gear marketplaces.

The hosted marketplace and its payment features remain under active testing and may not be available in every browser, platform, or application build.

### MiniPay-compatible marketplace

A MiniPay-compatible version of the hosted marketplace is under testing.

When enabled and opened through the supported MiniPay environment, users may be able to complete eligible purchases using supported Celo stablecoins.

MiniPay checkout should not be treated as the default payment method inside the native Move+ application.

Detailed payment availability is documented separately under Marketplace Payments.

## Product categories

Marketplace listings may include selected fitness and lifestyle categories such as:

- Wearables.
- Apparel.
- Fitness accessories.
- Nutrition-related products.
- Recovery products.
- Vouchers.
- Move+ merchandise.
- Approved partner products.

The appearance of a category does not guarantee that products are currently available within it.

Move+ Marketplace is currently focused on fitness-related and ecosystem-related products rather than unrestricted general commerce.

## Real-item marketplace

The native Move+ marketplace allows eligible users to redeem supported physical items using Energy.

A typical real-item flow may include:

1. Opening the marketplace.
2. Browsing currently available products.
3. Selecting a product.
4. Reviewing its details and Energy requirement.
5. Confirming that sufficient Energy is available.
6. Providing the required delivery information.
7. Submitting the order.
8. Waiting for fulfillment confirmation or further contact.

A successful checkout records the order for fulfillment. It does not necessarily mean that the item has already been packed, shipped, or delivered.

Detailed product, availability, and redemption information is maintained in the separate Real Items documentation.

## Digital Gear marketplace

Digital Gear may appear in hosted marketplace previews.

Current previews may include supported gear associated with:

- Ronin.
- Base.
- Genesis collections.
- Base Founder Gear.

Some Digital Gear pages may provide filtering, collection information, or external marketplace links.

Digital Gear previews do not currently provide direct MiniPay purchasing, NFT minting, wallet signing, or asset transfer inside the Move+ marketplace.

Users who acquire compatible Digital Gear externally may link and verify it through the supported Move+ Digital Gear flow.

Cycling Digital Gear remains under development and should not be treated as currently available for purchase or use.

Detailed Digital Gear marketplace behavior is documented separately.

## Payment methods

Marketplace payment availability depends on the surface being used.

### Energy

Energy use for supported Real Items depends on the user’s eligibility.

Users with an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription may be eligible to pay the full cost of an eligible Real Item using Energy.

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may apply Energy only as a discount of up to 20% toward an eligible stablecoin checkout. At least 80% remains payable using a supported stablecoin when the maximum discount is applied.

Users generally need:

- A valid Move+ account.
- Sufficient Energy.
- The applicable subscription or verified Digital Gear eligibility.
- An eligible and available product.
- Complete delivery information where required.

Full Energy payment does not require a blockchain wallet.

A Digital Gear holder discount requires verified compatible gear and the matching linked wallet.

Exact Energy conversion calculations are not published.

Energy does not represent guaranteed monetary value, cash, or a transferable payment balance.

### MiniPay and Celo stablecoins

The hosted marketplace may support selected Celo stablecoin payments through MiniPay when the checkout feature is enabled and opened in the correct MiniPay environment.

This integration remains under testing.

Supported tokens, checkout requirements, payment verification, and limitations are maintained in the separate Payments documentation.

### Energy-linked discounts

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may apply Energy as a discount of up to 20% toward an eligible hosted stablecoin checkout.

At least 80% of the checkout remains payable using a supported stablecoin when the maximum discount is applied.

This discount may depend on:

- A linked Move+ account.
- A linked and verified compatible wallet.
- Verified supported Digital Gear ownership.
- Sufficient Energy.
- Product eligibility.
- Current checkout configuration.

Full Energy payment remains limited to eligible active iOS subscribers.

Exact Energy conversion calculations and internal discount formulas are documented separately and are not published on this page.

## Accounts and wallets

A Move+ account is required for supported Energy use.

Eligible active iOS subscribers may complete a qualifying full-Energy Real Item checkout without a blockchain wallet.

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders require the matching linked wallet to receive an Energy discount of up to 20% toward an eligible stablecoin checkout.

A supported wallet may be required when the user chooses to:

- Verify Digital Gear ownership.
- Open or interact with external Digital Gear marketplaces.
- Use Ronin or Base Digital Gear utility.
- Complete supported blockchain or stablecoin transactions.

MiniPay checkout requires access through the compatible MiniPay environment.

Guest Energy checkout is not presented as a supported public marketplace flow.

## Orders

After an eligible order is submitted, it may enter a pending or fulfillment-related state.

Depending on the payment method and marketplace surface, users may see messages such as:

- Order submitted.
- Payment pending.
- Payment verified.
- Pending fulfillment.
- Payment failed or expired.
- Order cancelled.

Move+ does not currently provide a complete public shipment-tracking system with detailed processing, shipped, and delivered milestones for every order.

Users may be contacted when additional fulfillment or delivery coordination is required.

## Delivery and fulfillment

Physical-item delivery is currently limited to supported addresses within the Philippines.

Users may be asked to provide:

- Recipient name.
- Contact number.
- Email address.
- Complete delivery address.
- Other information required for fulfillment.

Physical orders may use manually coordinated fulfillment.

Marketplace confirmation does not guarantee immediate shipment or a specific delivery date.

International and broader Southeast Asian delivery are not currently available.

Detailed delivery, fulfillment, and order-handling information is maintained in the separate Delivery and Fulfillment documentation.

## Product availability

Products may become unavailable because of:

- Limited stock.
- Offer expiration.
- Fulfillment restrictions.
- Product removal.
- Marketplace maintenance.
- Payment-method limitations.
- Geographic delivery restrictions.

A product being visible in the marketplace does not guarantee that checkout or delivery will remain available.

## External Digital Gear marketplaces

Some Digital Gear previews may direct users to an external blockchain marketplace.

External marketplace transactions are separate from Move+ real-item Energy checkout.

Before approving an external blockchain transaction, users should verify:

- The connected wallet.
- The selected network.
- The asset collection.
- The marketplace address or application.
- The transaction details.
- Applicable network fees.

Move+ does not guarantee the availability, liquidity, resale value, or transaction outcome of externally listed Digital Gear.

## Current limitations

- Real-item Energy redemption is primarily available through the native Move+ marketplace.
- Full Energy payment is limited to eligible users with an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription.
- Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may use Energy only for a discount of up to 20%.
- At least 80% of a Digital Gear holder checkout remains payable using a supported stablecoin when the maximum discount is applied.
- Exact Energy conversion calculations are not published.
- The hosted marketplace remains under active testing.
- MiniPay and Celo stablecoin checkout are not enabled in every environment.
- Full Energy payment is not generally available through the hosted marketplace.
- Digital Gear marketplace pages are currently preview-oriented.
- Direct MiniPay NFT purchasing, minting, signing, and transfer are not available.
- Cycling Digital Gear is not currently available through the marketplace.
- Physical-item delivery is limited to the Philippines.
- International delivery is not currently available.
- Complete user-facing shipment tracking is not yet available.
- Merchant self-onboarding is not currently available.
- Product availability, stock, and payment methods may change.
- Energy and Digital Gear do not guarantee financial or resale value.

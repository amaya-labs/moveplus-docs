# Real Items

**Status:** Public documentation draft

Real Items are the physical products and vouchers offered through Move+ Marketplace.

The Real Items catalog is focused on fitness, active lifestyle, Move+ merchandise, and selected approved partner products.

Move+ Marketplace is not currently an open seller platform. Products are selected and managed through the Move+ marketplace system.

## Current availability

Real Items can currently appear through:

- The native Move+ Marketplace.
- The hosted Move+ Marketplace.
- The MiniPay-compatible hosted marketplace while that integration is under testing.

The native Move+ Marketplace is the primary surface for eligible full-Energy Real Item redemption.

Full Energy payment is limited to users with an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription.

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may use Energy only as a discount of up to 20% toward an eligible stablecoin checkout.

The hosted marketplace provides catalog browsing, product details, cart functionality, and selected payment features where enabled.

Product availability, stock, price, category, and payment options may change.

## Product categories

Real Items may appear under categories such as:

- Wearables.
- Apparel.
- Fitness accessories.
- Nutrition-related products.
- Recovery products.
- Vouchers.
- Move+ merchandise.
- Approved partner products.

The presence of a category does not guarantee that products are currently available within it.

Cycling Digital Gear and blockchain-based Digital Gear are documented separately and are not treated as Real Items.

## Browsing products

Users may be able to:

- Browse currently available products.
- Filter products by supported category.
- Open product details.

- Review stablecoin pricing where available on the hosted marketplace.
- Check whether a product is available, limited, expired, or sold out.

- Add supported products to a hosted shopping cart.

Marketplace images may be illustrative. Users should review the available product information before submitting an order.

A Real Item listing may include:

## Energy use

Energy use for Real Items depends on the user’s eligibility.

### Full Energy payment

Users with an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription may be eligible to redeem the full cost of a supported Real Item using Energy.

A typical full-Energy redemption flow is:

1. Sign in to Move+.
2. Confirm that the qualifying iOS subscription is active.
3. Open Marketplace.
4. Browse eligible Real Items.
5. Open the intended product.
6. Review the Energy requirement.
7. Confirm that sufficient Energy is available.
8. Enter the required delivery information.
9. Submit the redemption.
10. Receive confirmation that the order was recorded.
11. Wait for fulfillment coordination.

Full Energy payment generally requires:

- A valid Move+ account.
- An active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription.
- Sufficient Energy.
- An available and eligible product.
- Complete delivery information where required.
- Successful marketplace validation.

A blockchain wallet is not required for eligible full-Energy payment.

### Digital Gear holder discount

Users with verified compatible Digital Gear may apply Energy only as a discount toward an eligible stablecoin checkout.

The discount is limited to a maximum of 20% of the eligible checkout value.

At least 80% of the checkout value remains payable using a supported stablecoin when the maximum discount is applied.

This discount generally requires:

- A valid Move+ account.
- A linked wallet for the compatible Digital Gear network.
- Verified supported Digital Gear ownership.
- Sufficient Energy.
- An eligible Real Item.
- An active supported stablecoin checkout.
- Complete delivery information.

Digital Gear ownership alone does not provide full Energy payment.

Exact Energy conversion calculations are not published.

Energy is an in-app reward and should not be treated as cash, a stablecoin, or a guaranteed transferable payment balance.

## Native marketplace behavior

The native Move+ Marketplace currently supports single-product full-Energy redemption for eligible active iOS subscribers.

The native marketplace may provide:

- Product browsing.
- Category filtering.
- Product detail views.
- Energy price sorting.
- Eligibility validation for supported full-Energy redemption.
- Product availability messaging.
- Delivery information entry.
- Order confirmation.

A multi-item cart and quantity controls are not currently part of the standard native Energy redemption flow.

## Hosted marketplace

The hosted Move+ Marketplace may provide:

- Real Item browsing.
- Search.
- Category filters.
- Product detail views.
- Cart functionality.
- Quantity controls.
- Availability filtering.
- Digital Gear holder Energy discounts of up to 20% where supported.
- MiniPay-compatible stablecoin checkout where enabled.

The hosted marketplace remains under active testing.

Full Energy payment is not generally available through the hosted marketplace.

Full Energy redemption remains limited to eligible active iOS subscribers through the supported native Move+ flow.

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may use Energy only for a discount of up to 20%, with at least 80% remaining payable through a supported stablecoin when the maximum discount is applied.

## MiniPay-compatible checkout

A MiniPay-compatible Real Items checkout experience is under testing.

When enabled and opened through the supported MiniPay environment, eligible products may be purchased using supported Celo stablecoins.

The user may be required to:

- Open the marketplace inside MiniPay.
- Review the selected product or cart.
- Provide delivery information.
- Review the stablecoin amount.
- Approve the transaction through the MiniPay wallet.
- Wait for payment verification.
- Confirm the resulting order status.

MiniPay checkout should not be treated as fully available in every browser, platform, country, or application build.

Detailed token support, payment verification, Energy discounts, and checkout limitations are documented separately under Payments.

## Digital Gear holder Energy discount

Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may apply Energy as a discount toward an eligible hosted stablecoin checkout.

The discount is limited to a maximum of 20% of the eligible checkout value.

At least 80% remains payable using a supported stablecoin when the maximum discount is applied.

Eligibility may depend on:

- A linked Move+ account.
- A linked wallet for the supported Digital Gear network.
- Verified compatible Digital Gear ownership.
- Sufficient Energy.
- Product eligibility.
- Active Marketplace availability.
- Current checkout configuration.

Digital Gear ownership does not provide full Energy payment.

Exact Energy conversion calculations and internal discount formulas are not published.

## Accounts and eligibility

A Move+ account is required for supported Energy use.

Full Energy payment additionally requires an active qualifying iOS Move+ subscription.

A Digital Gear holder discount additionally requires verified compatible Digital Gear ownership and the matching linked wallet.

Hosted Real Items may be browsed without completing a purchase.

MiniPay stablecoin checkout requires access to a compatible MiniPay wallet environment.

A linked Move+ account may be required when applying an Energy-linked discount.

Subscription ownership and Digital Gear ownership are not currently required simply to browse Real Items.

Guest Energy redemption is not presented as a supported public marketplace flow.

## Stock and product availability

Real Item availability may depend on:

- Tracked stock.
- Limited quantities.
- Offer expiration.
- Product activation.
- Fulfillment capacity.
- Geographic delivery limits.
- Payment-method availability.
- Marketplace maintenance.

A product may be visible but unavailable for checkout.

Products may become unavailable, expire, sell out, or be removed without remaining permanently available.

Move+ does not guarantee continuous stock or availability.

## Order confirmation

After a successful Energy redemption or verified MiniPay payment, the marketplace may show confirmation that the order was recorded.

An order confirmation does not necessarily mean that the product has already been:

- Packed.
- Shipped.
- Delivered.
- Completed.
- Collected by a courier.

Orders may remain pending while Move+ reviews the order and coordinates fulfillment.

## Order status

Depending on the marketplace surface and payment method, users may see order or checkout messages such as:

- Order submitted.
- Awaiting payment.
- Payment pending.
- Payment verified.
- Pending fulfillment.
- Payment failed.
- Payment expired.
- Order cancelled.
- Order completed.

Move+ does not currently provide a complete customer-facing shipment tracker with detailed processing, shipped, and delivered milestones for every order.

Users may be contacted through the information provided during checkout when additional fulfillment coordination is needed.

## Delivery information

Physical-item checkout may request:

- Recipient name.
- Contact number.
- Email address.
- Complete delivery address.
- Delivery comments or additional instructions.

Users are responsible for reviewing their delivery information before submitting the order.

Incorrect or incomplete information may delay or prevent fulfillment.

Detailed delivery and order-handling information is documented separately under Delivery and Fulfillment.

## Geographic availability

Physical-item delivery is currently limited to supported addresses within the Philippines.

International delivery is not currently available.

Broader Southeast Asian delivery remains a future direction and should not be treated as an active marketplace service.

Some vouchers or future non-physical products may follow different fulfillment rules where explicitly stated.

## Fulfillment

Real Item fulfillment may be manually coordinated by Move+.

This may include:

- Confirming the submitted order.
- Reviewing product availability.
- Contacting the recipient.
- Coordinating packing and delivery.
- Marking an order as completed after fulfillment.

Move+ does not currently provide a public courier integration or guaranteed delivery schedule for every product.

Marketplace confirmation does not guarantee immediate shipment or delivery by a specific date.

## Failed orders

A checkout may fail or remain incomplete when:

- The Energy balance is insufficient.
- The product is unavailable.
- The offer has expired.
- Required delivery information is missing.
- The MiniPay transaction is rejected.
- The payment session expires.
- Payment verification is incomplete.
- Marketplace functionality is unavailable.

Users should avoid submitting duplicate blockchain payments while a transaction is still pending.

Where available, users should review the payment status before beginning a new checkout.

## Cancellations and refunds

Move+ does not currently provide a complete self-service cancellation or refund system for all Real Item orders.

Cancellation, replacement, Energy restoration, or stablecoin refund availability should not be assumed unless Move+ confirms it for the specific order.

Automatic stablecoin refunds and automatic Energy restoration are not currently presented as standard public marketplace features.

Any applicable order issue should be raised through an official Move+ support channel.

Detailed policies may be added separately as the marketplace fulfillment system develops.

## Partner products

Move+ Marketplace may include products from approved partners.

Partner products appear only when selected and listed through the Move+ marketplace system.

The marketplace does not currently provide:

- Public seller registration.
- Merchant self-onboarding.
- Public seller dashboards.
- Open product listing by third parties.
- Public commission or settlement tools.

The presence of a partner product does not imply that all products from that partner are supported by Move+.

## Marketplace safety

Users should:

- Review product details before checkout.
- Confirm the Energy or stablecoin amount.
- Review the delivery address.
- Confirm product availability.
- Use only official Move+ marketplace surfaces.
- Review MiniPay transaction details carefully.
- Avoid submitting duplicate payments.
- Contact official Move+ support when an order requires assistance.

Blockchain payments are generally irreversible after confirmation.

Energy does not represent guaranteed money or cash value.

Full Energy payment is limited to eligible active iOS subscribers. Verified compatible Digital Gear holders may use Energy only for a discount of up to 20% toward a supported stablecoin checkout.

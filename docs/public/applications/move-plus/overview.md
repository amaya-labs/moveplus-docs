# Move+

**Status:** Public documentation draft

Move+ is a fitness-first mobile application that connects real-world activity with in-app rewards, Digital Gear progression, community features, and marketplace access.

The application is built for Android and iOS. Users can create a standard Move+ account and use the core fitness experience without connecting a blockchain wallet.

## What Move+ does

Move+ provides tools for users to:

- Track walking and running activities using GPS.
- Review distance, activity summaries, and weekly progress.
- Earn in-app Energy from eligible activities.
- Link optional Digital Gear on supported blockchain networks.
- Access marketplace features for Digital Gear and selected physical items.
- Join community discussions, challenges, raffles, and progression features.
- Track mileage associated with physical shoes.

Feature availability and reward eligibility may depend on the user’s account, subscription status, linked Digital Gear, application version, and active feature configuration.

Subscription access is currently available on iOS. Android does not yet offer a subscription, so feature eligibility may differ between platforms.

## Activity modes

### Walking and running

Walking and running tracking are active parts of the Move+ application.

Eligible sessions may produce Energy rewards after activity validation. Exact earning rules, daily limits, and eligibility requirements are documented separately and may change as the reward system develops.

### Cycling

Move+ includes a dedicated cycling experience that is currently under testing and continued development.

Cycling can display locally calculated activity information. During this testing phase, cycling activity saving and Energy rewards are not yet fully released.

## Energy and rewards

Energy is an in-app reward used within the Move+ ecosystem.

Energy does not represent guaranteed monetary value, guaranteed token value, or a guaranteed right to cash withdrawal. Reward availability and future utility depend on active application rules and supported features.

Detailed reward, progression, and eligibility information is maintained in the separate Rewards and Progression documentation.

## Optional Digital Gear

Digital Gear is an optional part of Move+.

Users do not need a blockchain wallet to access the basic fitness experience. Users who choose to participate in Web3 features may link supported Digital Gear and access compatible gear management and progression features.

Move+ currently supports optional Digital Gear linking on Ronin and Base.

Digital Gear behavior, durability, progression, multipliers, repair, and upgrade features are documented separately.

## Marketplace connection

Move+ includes an in-app marketplace experience connected to the wider Move+ ecosystem.

Marketplace functionality may include:

- Digital Gear visibility and access.
- Selected physical fitness-related items.
- Energy-based checkout for supported products.
- Delivery of eligible physical orders within the Philippines.

Some payment methods, hosted marketplace experiences, and checkout integrations remain feature-controlled or under testing.

Detailed marketplace information is maintained under the separate Move+ Marketplace documentation section.

## Community and progression

Move+ also includes community and engagement features such as:

- Community posts and messaging.
- Group discussions.
- Challenges and raffle experiences.
- Achievements and profile progression.
- Weekly activity summaries.
- Physical shoe mileage tracking.

Availability may vary by platform, account eligibility, and application version.

## Current development status

Move+ remains under active development and testing.

Some features are fully available, while others may be released gradually, limited to testing builds, or controlled through application configuration.

Amaya L1 integration is a planned infrastructure direction and is not currently presented as a live Move+ settlement or reward network.

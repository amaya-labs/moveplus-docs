# Digital Gear

**Status:** Public documentation draft

Digital Gear is the optional Web3 equipment layer of Move+.

Users can use the core Move+ fitness experience without connecting a blockchain wallet or owning Digital Gear. Users who choose to participate may link supported gear to their Move+ account and access compatible reward, durability, repair, and progression features.

Digital Gear is designed as fitness and application utility. It should not be treated as an investment, a guaranteed source of rewards, or a promise of financial return.

## Optional Web3 participation

A normal Move+ account does not require a blockchain wallet.

Wallet connection is only required when a user wants to:

- Verify ownership of supported Digital Gear.
- Display owned gear inside Move+.
- Select or equip compatible gear.
- Use gear-related reward eligibility.
- Access supported durability, repair, or progression features.
- Manage compatible marketplace or ownership links.

Users should only connect wallets they control and should never share a recovery phrase or private key with Move+ or another person.

## Supported networks

Move+ currently supports Digital Gear linking on:

- Ronin
- Base

The user chooses the appropriate network when beginning the gear-linking process.

Digital Gear from one network is verified and managed within its corresponding network context. Unsupported assets or collections may not appear inside Move+ even when they are held by the connected wallet.

Amaya L1 integration remains a planned infrastructure direction and is not currently a live Digital Gear network.

## Linking and verifying Digital Gear

The general linking flow is:

1. Open the Digital Gear or Web3 section in Move+.
2. Choose Ronin or Base.
3. Connect or create a compatible wallet through the available onboarding flow.
4. Allow Move+ to verify supported gear associated with the wallet.
5. Refresh or sync the Digital Gear dashboard.
6. Select compatible gear for use inside the application.

Move+ verifies wallet and gear ownership before providing gear-related utility.

Wallet verification does not transfer ownership of the asset to Move+. Blockchain assets remain associated with the user’s wallet unless the user performs a separate onchain transaction through an external wallet or supported marketplace.

## Selecting and using gear

Compatible Digital Gear can be displayed and selected inside the Move+ Web3 dashboard.

An active gear item may affect:

- Eligibility to earn Energy from supported activities.
- Applicable reward multipliers.
- Daily earning eligibility or limits.
- Durability or condition.
- Distance and progression records.
- Access to compatible repair or upgrade features.

Exact earning values, multipliers, limits, and economy rules are documented separately under Rewards and Progression.

Owning Digital Gear does not guarantee an Energy reward. Activities must still meet validation and eligibility requirements.

## Ronin Digital Gear

Move+ supports compatible Ronin shoe collections, including Genesis and Season gear.

Depending on the specific collection and current release configuration, Ronin Digital Gear may support:

- Ownership verification.
- In-app display.
- Gear selection or equipping.
- Activity reward eligibility.
- Durability tracking.
- Repair.
- Level, rarity, upgrade, or awakening-related progression.

Genesis and Season gear do not necessarily follow identical progression rules.

Some actions may depend on the gear type, current level, condition, active application configuration, and available network functionality.

## Base Founder Gear

Move+ supports the Base Founder Gear collection, which contains 100 numbered gear items.

Compatible Base Founder Gear may support:

- Wallet ownership verification.
- In-app display.
- Gear selection or equipping.
- Activity reward eligibility.
- Durability tracking.
- Repair.
- Distance and level progression.

Some Base Founder Gear progression features, including awakening-related actions, remain under development and are not yet fully released.

Base onboarding may use a hosted wallet experience. Available connection methods can differ by platform and application version.

## Durability and condition

Some Digital Gear includes durability or condition.

Activity use may reduce gear condition over time. Gear that falls below the required condition may no longer provide its normal earning utility until it is repaired.

The application may display:

- Current durability.
- Repair availability.
- Progress toward the next level.
- Distance recorded while the gear is active.
- Gear-specific progression information.

Exact durability requirements, wear rules, repair costs, and progression formulas are maintained in the Rewards and Progression documentation.

## Repair and progression

Supported gear can provide access to repair and progression features.

Depending on the network and gear type, these features may include:

- Restoring durability.
- Increasing a gear level.
- Progressing through distance milestones.
- Upgrading rarity.
- Awakening eligible gear.
- Refreshing supported onchain metadata.

Some repair and progression actions may require ENR where that functionality is enabled.

Availability varies by network, gear collection, application version, gear condition, and active release configuration. Not every action is available for every Digital Gear item.

## Energy and ENR

Energy is an in-app reward associated with eligible Move+ activity.

ENR is a separate Web3 utility asset used by supported gear-related features where enabled.

Neither Energy nor Digital Gear guarantees monetary value, token conversion, cash withdrawal, or financial return.

Detailed earning, conversion, repair, and progression rules belong in the Rewards and Progression documentation.

## Marketplace connection

Digital Gear may also appear in Move+ marketplace-related experiences.

Depending on the collection and marketplace surface, users may be able to:

- View supported Digital Gear.
- Filter gear by network.
- Open external ownership or marketplace pages.
- Link externally acquired gear for verification and use inside Move+.

Some marketplace displays are preview-only and do not provide direct in-app purchasing, minting, or signing.

Detailed listing, purchase, external-link, and marketplace availability information is maintained under the separate Move+ Marketplace Digital Gear documentation.

## Cycling gear

Character, Bike, Helmet, Cycling Shoes, Bottle Water, and other cycling loadout slots belong to the separate Cycling Mode system.

These cycling-specific gear categories are not yet presented as fully released general Digital Gear functionality.

Their availability and utility will be documented separately as Cycling Mode develops.

## Security and ownership

Move+ confirms compatible wallet and Digital Gear ownership before enabling gear-related functionality.

Users should verify:

- That they selected the correct network.
- That they connected the intended wallet.
- That the asset belongs to a supported collection.
- That the gear remains available in the connected wallet.
- That any external transaction is reviewed carefully before approval.

Move+ will never request a wallet recovery phrase or private key.

Public blockchain ownership and transaction information may be visible through the relevant network.

## Current limitations

- Digital Gear is optional and is not required for basic fitness tracking.
- Only supported collections on Ronin and Base can be verified.
- Some onboarding methods may be platform-specific or feature-controlled.
- Reward eligibility still depends on activity validation.
- Exact earning multipliers and limits vary by gear and active rules.
- Some upgrade and awakening features remain under development.
- Move+ does not guarantee liquidity, resale, token conversion, or cash-out for Digital Gear.
- Some marketplace Digital Gear views are preview-only.
- Cycling-specific gear remains under development.
- Amaya L1 is not currently a live Digital Gear network.

# Rewards and Progression

**Status:** Public documentation draft

Move+ connects eligible fitness activity with in-app Energy rewards, Digital Gear utility, and progression features.

Tracking an activity does not automatically guarantee a reward. Energy is finalized only after the activity, account, and any linked Digital Gear meet the applicable eligibility and validation requirements.

Energy, ENR, and Digital Gear should not be treated as investments, guaranteed sources of income, or promises of monetary value.

## Energy

Energy is an in-app reward used within the Move+ ecosystem.

Depending on currently available features, Energy may support:

- Eligible walking and running rewards.
- Selected marketplace purchases.
- Challenges, raffles, or engagement features.
- Progression-related activity.
- Conversion into ENR when conversion is enabled and the user qualifies.

Energy is not a bank balance, stablecoin, or guaranteed payment.

Move+ does not guarantee:

- A fixed amount of Energy for every activity.
- Permanent earning rates.
- Conversion availability.
- Token value.
- Liquidity.
- Cash withdrawal.

## Tracking and earning

Users can track supported activities even when they are not eligible to earn Energy.

Walking and running may qualify for Energy when the activity is successfully completed, validated, and associated with an eligible account or compatible active Digital Gear.

Eligibility may depend on:

- Activity type.
- Recorded distance.
- Activity validation.
- Account status.
- Platform availability.
- Active subscription status where supported.
- Linked and equipped Digital Gear.
- Gear condition.
- Daily earning limits.
- Current application and reward configuration.

The ability to record an activity should not be interpreted as a guarantee that the activity will earn Energy.

## iOS subscription and Android access

Subscription access is currently available on iOS.

An active and valid iOS subscription may provide access to supported earning and application benefits according to the current subscription configuration.

Android does not currently offer a Move+ subscription.

Android users can still use supported activity-tracking features. They may also qualify for Energy through compatible linked Digital Gear when the current reward rules allow it.

Subscription pricing, trial availability, and included benefits may change and should be confirmed through the application and the relevant platform store.

## Digital Gear eligibility

Compatible Digital Gear can affect reward eligibility and progression.

Depending on the gear collection and network, Digital Gear may affect:

- Whether an activity qualifies for Energy.
- Applicable earning multipliers.
- Daily earning limits.
- Durability or condition.
- Distance and level progression.
- Access to repair, upgrade, or awakening features.

Gear generally needs to be:

- Owned by the connected wallet.
- Successfully verified by Move+.
- Supported by the application.
- Selected or equipped where required.
- In an eligible condition.
- Used with a supported activity mode.

Owning Digital Gear alone does not guarantee a reward.

Exact multipliers, rarity rules, caps, and progression formulas may vary and are not guaranteed to remain permanent.

## Activity validation

Rewards are finalized only after activity and account eligibility checks.

Move+ may review the consistency and completeness of recorded activity information before adding Energy to the user’s balance.

An activity may earn less Energy or no Energy when:

- Required activity conditions are not met.
- Location or movement information is incomplete.
- The activity contains inconsistent or suspicious information.
- Daily earning eligibility has already been reached.
- Linked Digital Gear is unsupported, inactive, or below the required condition.
- The user does not meet the current reward eligibility rules.

Move+ does not publish its internal validation thresholds, security rules, or abuse-detection methods.

## Daily earning limits

Move+ applies daily earning limits.

Limits may depend on:

- Account eligibility.
- Subscription status where supported.
- Digital Gear collection.
- Gear rarity.
- Gear level.
- Gear condition.
- Active multiplier rules.
- Current reward configuration.

Daily limits may be measured using eligible activity distance, Energy, or a combination of reward-system conditions.

Move+ may also apply progressive activity requirements during the same earning day.

Exact limits and reset boundaries may change as the reward economy is reviewed and balanced.

## Durability and wear

Some Digital Gear uses durability or condition.

Eligible activity may gradually reduce the condition of the active gear.

Gear that falls below the required condition may:

- Lose part of its normal utility.
- Stop providing reward eligibility.
- Require repair before it can be used again for supported earning.

Durability behavior may differ between Ronin gear, Base Founder Gear, and future gear collections.

Cycling loadout durability shown in the current Cycling Mode interface is not yet functional Digital Gear durability.

## Repair

Compatible Ronin and Base Digital Gear may support repair using ENR where that functionality is enabled.

Repair can restore the condition of eligible gear and allow it to resume its supported application utility.

Repair availability may depend on:

- Network.
- Gear collection.
- Current condition.
- Connected wallet.
- Available ENR balance.
- Application version.
- Active backend and network functionality.

Exact repair prices, formulas, settlement behavior, and network costs are not published on this page.

## Levels and distance progression

Some Digital Gear can progress through activity distance and level milestones.

Progression may include:

- Recorded distance while the gear is active.
- Level increases.
- Access to additional progression stages.
- Rarity upgrades.
- Awakening-related features.
- Updated Digital Gear information.

Progression behavior differs by collection.

Ronin Genesis, Ronin Season, and Base Founder Gear do not necessarily use identical rules.

## Ronin progression

Supported Ronin Digital Gear may include:

- Genesis and Season shoe utility.
- Distance and level progression.
- Durability and repair.
- Season rarity upgrades.
- Genesis awakening-related progression.
- Collection-specific reward multipliers and limits.

Availability depends on the specific gear, level, condition, and current release configuration.

## Base Founder Gear progression

Base Founder Gear may include:

- Ownership verification.
- Equipping.
- Activity reward eligibility.
- Durability and repair.
- Recorded distance.
- Level progress.

Some Base Founder Gear actions, including awakening-related progression, remain under development and are not yet fully released.

Ronin and Base progression should not be assumed to use the same economy or upgrade rules.

## ENR

ENR is a separate Web3 utility asset used by supported Move+ gear systems where enabled.

Current or supported ENR utility may include:

- Repairing eligible Ronin Digital Gear.
- Repairing eligible Base Founder Gear.
- Supporting selected upgrade or awakening actions.
- Other gear-progression utility as released.

Not every ENR-related action is available for every network or gear collection.

ENR does not carry a guaranteed price, liquidity level, resale market, conversion right, or cash value.

## Energy-to-ENR conversion

Move+ includes an Energy-to-ENR conversion experience that may be available when enabled.

Conversion may depend on:

- Current backend availability.
- Application configuration.
- Account eligibility.
- Compatible active Digital Gear.
- Minimum or maximum conversion requirements.
- Network-specific support.
- Additional reward and security checks.

The presence of a conversion interface does not guarantee that conversion is always available.

Exact conversion ratios and limits are not published on this page.

## ENR withdrawal

ENR withdrawal to a user-controlled wallet is not currently available through the released Move+ withdrawal experience.

A displayed ENR balance should not be interpreted as:

- A guaranteed right to withdraw.
- A guaranteed market value.
- A guarantee of liquidity.
- A promise of future cash redemption.

Any future withdrawal feature will require separate production verification, security review, and public documentation.

## Marketplace and other Energy utility

Energy can be used for supported marketplace purchases where Energy checkout is available.

This may include selected physical products offered through the Move+ Marketplace.

Other Energy-based experiences may include challenges, raffles, achievements, or engagement rewards. Availability and reward amounts may vary, and some promotional features may be testing-only or operationally limited.

Marketplace payment, fulfillment, and product rules are documented separately under Move+ Marketplace.

## Chain-specific differences

Move+ supports Digital Gear utility on Ronin and Base, but the two networks do not necessarily use identical rules.

### Ronin

Ronin Digital Gear may support:

- Genesis and Season shoe collections.
- Gear-based activity eligibility.
- Collection-specific multipliers and limits.
- Durability and repair.
- Level, rarity, upgrade, or awakening progression.
- Energy-to-ENR conversion when enabled.

### Base

Base Founder Gear may support:

- Gear-based activity eligibility.
- Durability and repair.
- Distance and level progression.
- Base-specific utility and configuration.

Some Base progression and conversion-related functionality may remain limited or under development.

Users should review the current in-app status before relying on a particular chain-specific feature.

## Cycling reward status

The dedicated Cycling Mode does not currently award real Energy.

It may display a local estimated Energy value during testing, but this estimate is not added to the user’s Energy balance.

Cycling-specific reward rules, Digital Gear combinations, water utility, distance caps, repair, and progression remain under development.

Planned cycling economy values should not be treated as current user rules.

## Achievements and progress features

Move+ may also provide non-token progression features such as:

- Weekly activity summaries.
- Personal running records.
- Achievements.
- Profile progression.
- Physical shoe mileage tracking.
- Challenges and raffle participation.

Some features may include Energy-related rewards or bonuses where operationally available.

Reward amounts and promotional conditions may change or be limited to specific campaigns.

## Current limitations

- Tracking does not guarantee Energy.
- Dedicated Cycling Mode does not currently award real Energy.
- Android does not currently offer a subscription.
- Energy eligibility can depend on active Digital Gear.
- Reward rates, multipliers, and daily limits may change.
- Low gear condition may prevent gear-based earning.
- Energy-to-ENR conversion may be unavailable when disabled.
- Exact conversion ratios and limits are not guaranteed.
- ENR withdrawal is not currently available.
- Ronin and Base do not necessarily use identical progression rules.
- Some upgrade and awakening features remain under development.
- Energy, ENR, and Digital Gear do not guarantee financial value or cash-out.
